# Beginner usability check

Use this check before visual direction work and again before presenting the direction for approval.

The reviewer should not know the design rationale. Hide annotations, concept names, and explanatory notes. Show only what a real user would see.

## First look

Show the first useful screen for five seconds, then hide it. Ask:

1. What does this product help you do?
2. What would you do first?
3. What do you expect to happen next?

Fail the check if the reviewer needs a spoken explanation, mistakes decoration for the main action, or cannot name the next step.

## Main task

Give the reviewer one goal in everyday language. Do not tell them which control to use.

Watch for:

- hesitation about where to start;
- labels they do not understand;
- choices that appear before the reviewer has enough information;
- hidden gestures or controls;
- uncertainty about whether an action worked;
- dead ends or a need for coaching.

The task passes when the reviewer reaches the intended result, understands what changed, and can say what they would do next.

## Mistake and recovery

Ask the reviewer to change, undo, cancel, or recover from one realistic mistake.

The recovery passes when they can find it, understand its effect, and return to a safe state without help.

## Content check

Review every visible sentence as if the person has never used this type of product.

- Use short, everyday words.
- Explain a necessary new term when it first appears.
- Name actions by their result.
- Remove text that does not help a decision or action.
- Do not rely on icons, color, or illustrations alone for essential meaning.

## Direction check

Run the test on the direction produced in this invocation. It must stand on its own and cannot depend on a previous direction or the designer's explanation.

If a fresh human reviewer is unavailable, use a reviewer with fresh context and no access to the design notes. Record that limitation. Do not claim that the product is proven intuitive until a suitable person completes the test.
